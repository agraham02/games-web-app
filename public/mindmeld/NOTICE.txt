vectors-v2.bin and vocab-v2.json — Mind Meld's word data

This data contains semantic vectors from ConceptNet Numberbatch, by Luminoso
Technologies, Inc. You may redistribute or modify the data under the terms of
the CC-By-SA 4.0 license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Source: ConceptNet Numberbatch 19.08, English
(https://github.com/commonsense/conceptnet-numberbatch), described in
Speer, Chin and Havasi (2017), "ConceptNet 5.5: An Open Multilingual Graph of
General Knowledge", AAAI 2017.

Changes made: reduced to about 15,000 common English words (the word list and
its frequency order came from GloVe 6B, public domain under the PDDL), centred
on their mean, scaled to unit length and stored as signed bytes. Built by
scripts/build-mindmeld-numberbatch.ts.

As a modified version of Numberbatch, these two files are themselves licensed
under CC BY-SA 4.0. This licence covers this data only, not the rest of the
application's code.
